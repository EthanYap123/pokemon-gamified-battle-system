How to Play

  1. ⚔️ Go to Team and select your starter Pokémon.
  2. 🪨 Try to beat Brock, the first Gym Leader.
  3. 💰 Visit the Shop and buy a new Pokémon.
  4. 📖 Check your Pokédex to see the updated entry.
  5. 👥 Explore Pokémon to view other users’ collections.
  6. ⚔️ Return to Team and add your new Pokémon to your lineup.
  7. 🏆 Challenge the Gyms and aim to become Champion!
